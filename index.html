Meet Your Designer

A boutique studio, run by one designer who cares about the details.

I'm Xander, a Senior Brand Identity Designer based in Cape Town, South Africa. I specialise in creating strategic logo and brand identity systems for businesses that want to present themselves clearly and professionally.

Over the past decade, I've worked across multiple industries including sports, automotive, wellness, and corporate brands, helping companies develop visual identities that are both meaningful and commercially effective. My focus is on clarity, consistency, and long-term brand value.

Our Process

Complete Identity Packages In Four Steps

Need a custom package? Let’s discuss your requirements.

01

Discover

Typically 1-2 Days

Once the 50% deposit has cleared, we start with a conversation and structured questionnaire to understand your goals, audience, positioning and vision.

02

Define

Typically 1-2 Weeks

Moodboarding and concepting continue until the creative direction and final logo are approved. Revisions also take place within this step.

03

Design

Typically 1-2 Weeks

With the logo approved, your full brand identity begins and you'll receive a complete first draft for review. Revisions also take place within this step.

04

Deliver

Typically 1-2 Days

Your completed brand files are prepared, organised and delivered in professional formats, ready for print, digital and social.

Timelines are estimates and may vary depending on feedback, approvals and project requirements.
